Escaloplab v80 printer firmware 1.7.4

- Firmware for every kind of v80: 220 or 300 bed, Bowden tube or direct drive. Your printer installs the right one by itself over Wi-Fi.
- Faster printing over Wi-Fi: files reach the SD card about twice as fast.

**How to update**
- With the Wi-Fi module: the printer shows "New firmware available": press Update.
- Over USB: Escalop Workshop, Printer tab, "Update its firmware over USB" (it picks the file for your printer).

Your settings (Z offset, leveling) are kept.
