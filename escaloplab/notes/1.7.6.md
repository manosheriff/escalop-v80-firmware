Escaloplab v80 printer firmware 1.7.6

- Printer 00003: Z steps back to 800 (it moved half the distance), also replacing the Z steps saved on the printer. Other printers: no change.

**How to update**
- With the Wi-Fi module: the printer shows "New firmware available": press Update when it isn't printing.
- Over USB: Escalop Workshop, Printer tab, "Update its firmware over USB" (it picks the file for your printer).

Your settings (Z offset, leveling) are kept.
