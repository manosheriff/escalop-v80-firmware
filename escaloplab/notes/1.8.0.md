Escaloplab v80 printer firmware 1.8.0

- Every v80 now uses A4988 stepper drivers on all axes, with all 3 jumpers under each driver (1/16 microstepping).
  Steps per mm: X 80, Y 80, Z 400, E 93. The firmware's steps now always replace the ones saved on the printer.
- A file the SD card couldn't open during a Wi-Fi upload is removed again (it used to stay as an empty file).
- The install screen warns that the printer's screen may go blank for about a minute.

**Before you update:** the printer must have A4988 drivers on X, Y, Z and E with all 3 jumpers fitted.
With DRV8825 drivers this firmware moves every axis half as far.

**How to update**
- With the Wi-Fi module: the printer shows "New firmware available": press Update when it isn't printing.
- Over USB: TripleF3D app, Printer tab, "Update its firmware over USB" (it picks the file for your printer).

Your other settings (Z offset, leveling) are kept.
